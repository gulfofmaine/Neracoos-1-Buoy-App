NERACOOS Mariners' Dashboard

Welcome to the NERACOOS Mariners' Dashboard, which delivers high-quality, timely data from a growing network of buoys and sensors into the hands of mariners heading to sea.

We've updated the Mariners' Dashboard to improve user experience while maintaining the trusted data and information you rely on. Please explore the updated version and share your feedback.

How to Use the Dashboard

  • To view the current conditions at a station, click a circle on the map
  • You can also filter stations for a specific area of the Northeast by clicking the Station List dropdown menu located in the upper right-hand corner of the page
  • On individual station details, use the "All Data" tab to view the most recent data for the station's available variables, and the "Forecasts" tab to view predicted conditions at the location
